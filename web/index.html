Frequently Asked Questions

Is this generator really free?

Yes. All features are free to use with no watermarks or registration. Everything runs in your browser, so your data never leaves your device.

Which barcode formats are supported?

Choose from popular symbologies including Code 128, EAN, UPC-A, Code 39, ITF-14, and MSI. We rely on the open-source JsBarcode library to ensure standards-compliant output.

How do I download the generated code?

Use the download buttons to export as PNG or SVG. For QR codes, we render to a canvas and convert it on the fly so you get crisp exports at any size.

Can I customize colors and sizes?

Absolutely. Adjust foreground/background colors, module width, height, and more before generating your code.
